What worked
A written standard, enforced by a test
AGENTS.md and the content test changed together; 96 workflow runs, none failed.
Reviewers from a different vendor than the builders
Codex found what Claude and the Keelson implementers missed, and the Claude peer found what Codex missed.
An approval gate where every plan was corrected before code
13 of 13 plans approved with specific corrections; none needed a second pause.
What did not
Media regenerated four times, then cut
Four orientation recordings in three hours on Friday; both introductory recordings removed on Sunday.
The workflow's internal review loop found almost nothing
12 clean verdicts and 1 wrong must-fix. Its value was isolation, planning, and durability.
Pull requests merged before the reviewer bot posted
Median 6 minutes from open to merge, about how long Copilot takes to post.
