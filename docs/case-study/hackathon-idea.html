Azure SPI Fieldnotes

An onboarding course that teaches engineers the Azure SPI machinery, built by a factory of AI agents from more than one vendor under one engineer's direction. Two deliverables: the site, and the record of how it was built.

Deliverable 1
A training site over an inspectable map

Seven lessons in one round trip. Each lesson is a set of claims; select a claim and the map focuses on the parts that prove it, with the evidence one drawer away.

Around the lessons: audio deep dives with source-check notes, a short video, hand-drawn field guides, posters. No quizzes, no scores.
Deliverable 2
A case study of the factory that builds it

One engineer acts as the manager, not the coder. Agents from more than one vendor do the building and the reviewing, and a shared record survives every session.

A written content standard, enforced by a test before every deploy, is the quality bar the factory is held to.
The question we want answered: can one engineer, acting as a manager rather than a coder, get something this size built and reviewed by AI agents from more than one vendor, with a durable shared record that survives every session boundary?
What we will measure. Human messages against agent turns. Workflow runs, what their review loop caught, and what the approval gate changed. Tracker discipline. Rework. Cost, as far as each vendor shows it.
