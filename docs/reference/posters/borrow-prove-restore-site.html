The verdict · pass needs borrow, prove, and restore all green
A suite passes
Zero exit, at least one test that was not skipped, and no failures or errors in the copied reports. A clean container exit alone proves nothing; zero tests collected is never a pass.
The lane fails
Any suite fails, verify times out or reports lock_mismatch, or restore exits 1. A pin refused while deployable fails at once; while not deployable it is retried for up to 10 min.
The lane skips, and says why
Outside-repository PR (no OIDC token), not onboarded, no descriptor, no image pushed, no suite declared. validation-summary stays green on a skip, so read the gate's reason.
Restore's two exits
Exit 2 means another run owns the pin, or none exists: treated as success. Exit 1 is a restore failure. A lost runner can still strand a pin; reset --ephemeral --stale-only exists to sweep it, by hand today.
