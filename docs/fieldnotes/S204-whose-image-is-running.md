# Fieldnote S204 · Whose image is running

**Status: published.** Level 200, the fourth episode of the service path. Its companion page is `chapters['whose-image']`, and its cut-from record is in `src/content/videos.js`. It teaches `image-lock` and `canonical-ages` and is home to the myth `refresh-is-automatic` (`src/content/curriculum.js`, `src/content/myths.js`).

**Who is watching:** an engineer on the service path who has brought up their own environment and changed a service. **What they should believe after it:** the image lock ties each service to one commit on its fork's `main` and one digest, which is what the pods run; that image ages toward GHCR retention, and only a refresh moves it, which on their own environment means they run `spi service refresh`.

**The scene:** footage first, from the take's `spi info` and `spi status`, with boards for what the take could not record (GHCR, the fork's commit, an image's age). Everything stays at level 200: the real commands and their output, and one failure, an image left to age. Retention's schedule and its numbers, canonical sources, trusted mirrors, the paired schema loader and the shared environment's refresh run belong to P303 and the platform path; the only numbers on screen are the ones the CLI prints in its own warning line.

**Made from:** Fieldnote 08 v1. The cold open's footage, claim 2's board and its take, and the easy mistake's take are kept; c1 loses one sentence (a splice of its own take), n4's closing answer is the last sentence of the old example's take, and n0, n1, n3, n5, n8 and n9 are new takes. The standfirst and the traced example are gone: the example retold claim 2 hop for hop.

## The companion page

The page `whose-image`, rewritten from this storyboard:

- Question: "Which commit does each service run?"
- Claim 1, "The image lock ties each service to one commit and one digest": "At spi up the CLI asks GHCR which digest main-snapshot names for each of thirteen forks and records it in the image lock under the sha tag of the commit that built it; a tag can move, so the lock keeps the digest and its build date, and the pods run the locked digest."
- Claim 2, "A canonical image ages toward retention until a refresh moves it": "Every image ages from the day it was built, and once it is old enough and main has built past it, GHCR retention can delete it, so spi info and spi status mark an old canonical, and only a refresh moves it."
- Example: the page keeps _Follow partition's tag home_, `spi info`, five hops from the lock entry to the pods. The episode traces partition's row inside the first chapter instead.
- Easy mistake: "My environment picks up the fork's new main on its own." (`refresh-is-automatic`, checked with `spi info`).
- Retention's schedule and numbers, canonical sources and trusted mirrors are P303's and stay off the claims; the drawer keeps the CLI's own 21-day mark.
