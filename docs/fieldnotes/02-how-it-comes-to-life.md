# Fieldnote 02 · How it comes to life

**Status: approved; the take is being recorded.** Cut from lesson 02 (`chapters['bring-up']`). Aim for about 2 minutes 40 seconds.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who has seen lesson 01 and is about to create an environment. **What they should believe after it:** `spi up` hands off to Flux rather than finishing the job; a returned command is not a usable API; and `spi down` keeps the names and identities while the data goes.

**The form is mixed: boards and real terminal footage.** The footage is one dated take of a fresh `spi up` into a throwaway environment, its readiness checks, and its `spi down`, recorded as described under _The take_ below. Footage shows what happened; boards explain it. A time on screen is either measured in the take, with its clock shown, or labeled illustrative.

**The scene:** boards are concept scenes in vector motion, in the site's colors and fonts. Footage sits in a terminal pane on the sheet, cropped to the lines that matter and zoomed to read at phone width, never a full-screen screenshot. Owner colors follow lesson 07's legend: CLI + Bicep, Flux, controllers. One generated illustration carries the recall. Everything stays at lesson 02's rung: the resource group, the cluster, and its namespaces as rooms; services are opaque boxes.

## Word list the narration must keep

- `spi up` and `spi down` are spoken "S P I up" and "S P I down"; CLI is "C L I".
- Flux starts before the CLI exits. There is no single moment when the CLI stops and Flux starts; the lanes overlap, and the board never draws a handoff line between them.
- `spi status --watch` follows rollout; it makes no API call. Only an authenticated request verifies an API path, and only the path it exercised.
- Teardown keeps the managed identities and the resource group, not the data. Kubernetes seed Secrets go with the cluster, so a rebuild gets new middleware passwords; the narration says "names", never "the same environment".
- Suspension stops fetching new commits. It does not stop Flux applying the cached revision or controllers running.
- Measured times belong to this take only and are labeled as such. Never add overlapping phase times into a total.
- Profiles, chart Secrets, and the CI teardown job are other stages' mistakes and stay out of this episode.
