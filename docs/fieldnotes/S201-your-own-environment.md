# Fieldnote S201 · Your own environment

**Status: published.** Level 200, the first episode on the service path. Its companion page is `chapters['your-environment']`, and its cut-from record is in `src/content/videos.js`; the curriculum entry is `S201` in `src/content/curriculum.js` (teaches `wait-for-deployable`, uses `stack-is-a-resource-group`, home of the myth `finished-means-ready`).

**Who is watching:** an engineer who has finished the foundations, chose the service path at the end of 105, and wants an environment of their own to work in. **What they should believe after it:** `spi check` comes first, and `spi up --env <name>` builds their stack as one resource group in their subscription; `spi up` returns while Flux is still reconciling, so they wait until `spi status` reads Deployable; and `spi info` names their endpoints, while `spi down` gives the environment back and keeps its managed identities for the next `spi up`.

**The scene:** footage-led. The service path take of 23 to 24 October 2026 carries every beat: `spi check`, `spi up` on the default branch, `spi status --watch` from Not deployable to Deployable, `spi info`, and `spi down`. Boards add only what a terminal does not draw: the take clock, the measured wait between the command returning and Deployable, and one Deployable lamp. Everything stays at level 200: real commands and their output, the usual path, and one failure (the command finished, and the summary still says Not deployable). No phases, Flux layers, maintenance, `--tag`, or how Ready and Deployable are computed; those are P201's.

**Made from:** a new take, with Fieldnote 02's footage idioms (the take clock, cuts labelled with the measured wait, the summary's count line cropped at `| SUSPENDED`) and its bookend beds. Fieldnote 02's boards are not reused: its owner lanes, ladder, stage hops and teardown board belong to P201 and P302, its request path to 102, and its easy mistake to P301 (the audit of 02). All narration is new.

## Take notes

- **Footage:** the service path take (`take-day-service-path/footage` in the video library), spi 0.32.1, osdu-spi-stack `main` at 448e0c6, profile core, `westus3`, default branch (no `--tag`, so no maintenance step). `spi check` exit 0 at 00:44:02Z on 24 October; `spi up` from 00:44:02Z to 01:21:33Z (37 min 31 s by `times.log`; the cast's last line is at 37 min 30 s); `spi status --json` read `ready` and `deployable` true together at 01:43:29Z (`status-poll.log`), 21 min 56 s after `spi up` returned and 59 min 27 s after it started. The watch cast prints `Deployable` at `+59:22` on the take clock, 21 min 52 s after the cast's return line; the board shows the cast's times, and the narration rounds to twenty-two minutes. `spi info` at 01:44:13Z; `spi down --env <name> --force` is part 3, recorded on 24 October with spi 0.32.2 (`5-down.cast`, 14:40Z to 14:54Z, exit 0): 14 min 20 s, the kubeconfig context removed at `+13:19`, and the group left holding five managed identities. `spi down --purge` ran off camera afterwards and removed the group; it is not shown. An earlier attempt the same evening failed while resolving images and was purged off camera (`aborted-1/`); it is not used.
- **Pointers:** "one resource group in your subscription" is the episode's only use of 102's idea; the board writes it as a chip and draws no resource-group frame. No line speaks an episode code.
- **What the board leaves out of the take:** the stage lines between the resource group and the return (phases, P201), the Settings table, the subscription line, the Kubernetes and Flux panels, the count line's `| SUSPENDED` (cropped and marked with an ellipsis; suspension is P301's), `Last spi up` timestamps, the gateway IP, the Azure and Partitions panels of `spi info`, and the Azure CLI and kubectl panels between `spi down`'s lines (they print the subscription id and every resource name). The count line is on screen in the summary and never spoken.
- **"deployable after convergence":** printed by `spi up` when the deploy records no maintenance (`src/spi/deploy.py`); with `--tag` it reads "maintenance enabled", which the default path never shows.
- **Not deployable reasons:** the watch's verdict line names the Kustomization holding it back; the board shows it as printed and the narration names none.
- **The easy mistake's evidence:** the watch's first summary, about six seconds after the command returned, reads `2/30` and `Not deployable`; the init Jobs ran later in the rollout. "Not that an API is ready" is the myth's correction; the narration does not claim Deployable proves an API call, since `spi status` makes none.
- **What spi down keeps:** one sentence, "It keeps your managed identities, so the next spi up reuses them." The teardown deletes everything in the group except the managed identities and leaves them standing with the group and its tags, "so a rebuild never rotates the deploy identity's client id" (`src/spi/teardown.py`); `--purge` deletes the group with them (`src/spi/cli.py`). The client id, the grants and `--purge` are P302's.
- **Masks:** the environment name as `<name>`, the signed-in account as `<you>`, the subscription id removed, the ingress host as `<api-host>`; no tenant, client or object id, token, home or temporary path survives in the extract.
- **Checked against:** osdu-spi-stack `origin/main` 448e0c6 on 23 October 2026: `spi check` (`src/spi/cli.py`, tools and Azure permissions), the return lines (`src/spi/cli.py`), the verdict (`src/spi/status.py`, "Deployable" or "Not deployable: "), and `spi down`'s confirmation and `--force` (`src/spi/cli.py`); `spi down`'s identity-preserving teardown re-checked on `origin/main` 44c44b8 on 24 October (`src/spi/teardown.py`, `src/spi/cli.py`).
