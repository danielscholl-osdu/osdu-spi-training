# Fieldnote 10 · Ready is not loaded

**Status: published, then replaced by S203.** Season 2, _Working the stack_. Cut from osdu-spi-stack ADR-030 (`docs/decisions/030-machine-readable-status-contract.md`), ADR-040 (`docs/decisions/040-named-loads.md`), PRs #303 and #304, `software/stacks/osdu/init/load-registry.yaml`, `src/spi/status.py`, `src/spi/loads.py`, `src/spi/deploy.py` and `src/spi/deploy_record.py`, read at `main` 870f729 (identical to the v0.32.0 tag 3d3106d outside `ops/`), and the 22 October take on spi 0.32.0. The lesson it proposes is `ready-not-loaded` in the _Working the stack_ book.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who has watched `spi status --watch` reach thirty of thirty (Fieldnote 02) and called the APIs as themselves (Fieldnote 09). **What they should believe after it:** Ready and Deployable are two verdicts, and Deployable needs more than Flux; a Deployable environment holds its schemas and no records; and `spi load` is a separate, recorded step whose `complete` means Storage accepted the records, not that search can find them.

**Money shot:** `spi load --status` before and after: `reference-data · absent` becoming `reference-data · complete · 80103`, on an environment that was Deployable the whole time.

**Form: mixed, footage-led.** The take recorded the whole sequence in order, so footage carries every claim: the maintenance verdict, `spi maintenance clear`, the Deployable summary, the empty load table, six minutes of `spi load`, and the full table. Boards carry the two things the terminal does not draw: which layers count toward Ready, and the path one batch takes into Storage.

**Length:** about 4 minutes (estimate below).

## Keep it true

- `ready` is Flux convergence of the gating Kustomizations. `spi-osdu-legal`, `spi-osdu-members` and `spi-osdu-dag-registration` carry `spi-stack.gating: "false"`: visible, never part of `ready`. `kustomizations.ready == total` is not the verdict; read the boolean.
- `deployable` is `ready` with `maintenance` unset, a deploy record present, and the `entitlements-members` Job Complete for every partition the init values name. `spi status` exits 0 only when Deployable, 2 when not.
- An environment brought up with `--tag` starts in maintenance (`initial_maintenance=bool(config.repo_tag)`); a branch environment is "deployable after convergence". Never say every new environment starts in maintenance. A rerun preserves the existing flag.
- Clearing maintenance is the operator's decision after readiness and probes; nothing in `spi up` clears it. The shared environment's lifecycle run sets and clears it itself.
- Schemas are a Flux Job (`runner: flux`) behind `spi-osdu-schema-load`, a gating layer, using the loader paired with schema's commit. `spi load` reports it and never creates it; `--force` recreates it.
- Reference data is `data/data-definitions` `v0.30.0`, pinned to commit `99f8fc88…`, fetched from community GitLab by commit on every Job run. No digest verifies the bytes.
- `spi load` refuses with `not_deployable` (exit 2) before a Job runner load unless the environment is Deployable, and refuses `requires_unmet` until `schemas` is `complete` or `stale`.
- The loader writes the partition's `data.default.owners` and `data.default.viewers` and its seeded legal tag in place of the manifest's, in batches of 500 with `skipdupes`; 200, 201 and 409 count as success. The Job fails when more than 1% of records fail, so `complete` can include up to 1% failed. The take had none.
- States are `absent`, `running`, `complete`, `stale`, `failed`. `complete` means Storage accepted the records; the indexer may still be working. A load never changes `ready` or `deployable`.
- Deleting a load Job removes the fact, not the records.
- The take's load took about six minutes; ADR-040 measured seven on its first run. The 7200 s deadline is headroom, not a measurement.

## Proposed lesson

`ready-not-loaded`, in the _Working the stack_ book. Map lesson at the namespaces-and-workloads rung: the Flux layers in `osdu-flux`, the schema and load Jobs and the Storage and Schema services in `osdu`, and the CLI on the workstation.

- **Title:** Ready is not loaded
- **Subhead:** Ready is Flux's verdict, Deployable adds the environment's own gates, and loading data is a separate step that changes neither.
- **Question:** When is a new environment ready, open for deploys, and holding data?
- **Builds on:** the bring-up and `spi status --watch` from lesson 02, and caller access from `who-is-asking`.
- **Outcomes (headlines):**
  1. Deployable is Ready, with maintenance off and bootstrap finished.
  2. A Deployable environment holds schemas and no records.
  3. Loaded means Storage accepted the records, not that search finds them.
- **Example:** _From Ready to 80,103 records_, `spi load`. Hops: `spi status` (ready, not deployable: maintenance) → `spi maintenance clear` (the deploy record's flag) → `spi load` (Deployable guard, `requires: [schemas]`) → the load Job (`load-reference-data-opendes-99f8fc88`, data-definitions at a pinned commit, 584 kinds checked against Schema) → Storage (80,103 accepted in batches of 500) → `spi load --status` (`complete`, 80103).
- **Easy mistake:** `deployable-means-loaded`, owner `cli`, theme `readiness`. Claim: "It's Deployable, so the data is there." Reality: bring-up registers schemas only; reference data is loaded by `spi load`, which runs after Deployable and never changes `ready` or `deployable`; `complete` means Storage accepted the records, and the indexer may still be working. Correction: "Deployable says an environment can take a deploy; spi load --status says what data it holds." Check: `spi load --status`. Sources: `statusContract` (ADR-030) and a new key for ADR-040.

## Unverified

- That the indexer was still working after `complete`, or that search returned the records at any point. ADR-040 states the gap; the take recorded no search. The episode draws it dashed and labeled.
- That a search suite on an unloaded environment "finds nothing". It is ADR-040's stated motivation, not a recorded run; n7 says "can find nothing".
