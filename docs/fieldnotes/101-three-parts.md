# Fieldnote 101 · Three parts

**Status: published.** Level 100, the first foundation episode. Its companion page is Start, whose "SPI means three things here" figure (`spiMeanings` in `src/content/concepts.js`) uses the same names. Start is not a lesson, so the episode has no chapters and no cut-from record.

**Who is watching:** anyone opening the course. **What they should believe after it:** Azure SPI is three parts that turn together: a stack that runs OSDU, an Azure provider inside each service, and an engineering system that keeps every service fork current; "SPI" can mean any of the three, and each lives in its own place.

**The scene:** the series emblem's three gears are the episode. They come apart to take their parts, gain where each part lives, and mesh again to show how a change moves between them. Each part is drawn in vector motion with the series icons; no scene shows the site's interface or its map drawings. No generated illustrations besides the notebook bookends.

**Made from:** Fieldnote 00. Its prequel, sting, and three-parts board are kept, with the third part named the engineering system; everything after that board is new. The provider is drawn in the fork's orange throughout, as in Fieldnote 00. Upstream history, the candidate test, and the stack's resources belong to later episodes (103, 104, 102).
