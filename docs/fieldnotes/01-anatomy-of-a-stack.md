# Fieldnote 01 · Anatomy of a stack

**Status: storyboard for approval.** Cut from lesson 01 (`chapters['running-stack']`) as it stands on `main`. Narration is about 390 words, about 2 minutes 40 seconds at 150 words a minute.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who knows OSDU and has just opened lesson 01. **What they should believe after it:** the stack is a resource group, not a cluster; some of its resources belong to a partition and some are shared; the services sit in one namespace behind one gateway.

**The scene:** every board is the lesson 01 map, `architectureOverview` in `src/components/architecture.js`, drawn at lesson 01's rung: the `osdu` namespace is one closed node, and the Git and image sources are not drawn. A board changes the map only the way the lesson does when a claim is selected: the claim's `focus` components at full weight, the rest receding, its `scopes` lit, boundaries never receding.

## Word list the narration must keep

- Development and test environment. Never production.
- The services run in one namespace; the board never opens it. Lesson 03 opens a service.
- The lookup reads stored configuration in common Storage and does not visit the partition's Cosmos DB, blob Storage, or Service Bus.
- `spi up` creates AKS and its Azure resources together. Flux and what happens after the command returns belong to Fieldnote 02.
- The environment name is `<name>` on screen and never spoken.
