# Fieldnote 01 · Anatomy of a stack

**Status: storyboard for approval, revised for motion after the pilot cut.** Cut from lesson 01 (`chapters['running-stack']`) as it stands on `main`. Narration is about 390 words, about 2 minutes 40 seconds at 150 words a minute.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who knows OSDU and has just opened lesson 01. **What they should believe after it:** the stack is a resource group, not a cluster; some of its resources belong to a partition and some are shared; the services sit in one namespace behind one gateway.

**The scene:** each beat is a concept scene drawn for the idea in vector motion, in the site's colors and fonts, with something always moving. Each claim and the example then settle for about two seconds onto the lesson 01 map, `architectureOverview` in `src/components/architecture.js`, with the claim selected as the lesson selects it, under an "On the lesson 01 map" chip, so the learner recognizes the drawing they will use. Three generated illustrations carry the standfirst, the easy mistake, and the recall; they assert nothing the lesson does not state, and every label on them is set as text, not generated. Everything stays at lesson 01's rung: the `osdu` namespace is one closed room, and services are opaque boxes.

## Word list the narration must keep

- Development and test environment. Never production.
- The services run in one namespace; the board never opens it. Lesson 03 opens a service.
- The lookup reads stored configuration in common Storage and does not visit the partition's Cosmos DB, blob Storage, or Service Bus.
- `spi up` creates AKS and its Azure resources together. Flux and what happens after the command returns belong to Fieldnote 02.
- The environment name is `<name>` on screen and never spoken.
