# Fieldnote 04 · The shape of the fork

**Status: published.** Cut on 23 October 2026, 2 minutes 56 seconds. Cut from lesson 04 (`chapters['fork-shape']`). Aim for about 3 minutes. The revision follows two changes since the first draft: upstream no longer carries the Azure provider, and every service the stack runs now has a fork of the reference fork's shape, whose `main` image the stack runs by default. The dated evidence is in the word list below. The three outcome sentences, the example, and the myth are unchanged.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who has just seen the Azure provider inside the partition service in Fieldnote 03. **What they should believe after it:** the fork owns a short list of paths and everything else is regenerated from upstream every day; three branches have three jobs; and the generated branch never carries the Azure paths, so an upstream deletion has nothing to delete, as the real one showed.

## Word list the narration must keep

- `fork_upstream` and `fork_integration` are spoken "fork upstream" and "fork integration".
- The example is illustrative from its first hop: the real cache fix arrived from upstream before the filter existed. The narration says so once, at the start.
- The fork owns paths, not the repository. Never say the fork "owns the code" without the path.
- Upstream does not carry the Azure provider; the fork's `main` does. Narration and boards say this as the end state, in the present tense, with no dates and no list of services, because those go stale. The evidence, as of 22 October 2026: the community partition repository removed `provider/partition-azure` and its Azure tests on 20 October (upstream `a2c4f7b7`, merging `de7e552f`, 79 files); the fork's next generated tree (`ec389615`) changed four shared files (`.mvn/community-maven.settings.xml`, `NOTICE`, `docs/docs/running.md`, `pom.xml`) and nothing under the fork-owned paths. Legal's removal merged the same day, schema's and file's on 22 October.
- `osdu-spi-partition` is the reference fork. As of 22 October 2026, thirteen service forks under the Azure organization (crs-catalog, crs-conversion, entitlements, file, indexer, indexer-queue, legal, partition, schema, search, storage, unit, workflow) each carry the three branches and a descriptor, and none has `provider/` on `fork_upstream`. The narration says "every OSDU service the stack runs has a fork of this shape" and names no count; it does not claim the others own the same paths, since a service laid out differently lists its own fork-owned trees.
- Since osdu-spi-stack 0.30.0, every service's default image is its fork's `main` build on GHCR. Say "by default": an environment can record a trusted mirror instead. Image refresh and age belong to Fieldnote 08.
- The nightly sync generates the new tree as a commit and proposes it to `fork_upstream` in a sync pull request, so the branch's tip is that pull request's merge. The trailer check searches for the generated commit rather than reading the tip.
- Mirror forks and the sync and cascade mechanics belong to Fieldnote 05.
