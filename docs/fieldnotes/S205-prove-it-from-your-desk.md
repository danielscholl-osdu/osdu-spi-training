# Fieldnote S205 · Prove it from your desk

**Status: published.** Level 200, the fifth episode of the service path. Its companion page is `chapters['prove-from-desk']`, and its cut-from record is in `src/content/videos.js`; the curriculum entry is `S205` in `src/content/curriculum.js` (teaches `suite-from-deployed-commit` and `local-change-as-pin`, uses `wait-for-deployable`, home to no myth).

**Who is watching:** an engineer on the service path who has a Deployable environment of their own (S201), a role in it (S202), and knows which commit each service runs (S204). **What they should believe after it:** `spi test <service>` runs the acceptance suite that the deployed commit shipped, against their own environment; a change on their desk reaches that environment as a pin built from their checkout, and `spi test --source` proves it only when the run is labeled matched; `spi service reset` puts the canonical image back.

**The scene:** footage first. Claim 1 is the take's `spi test partition` on the deployed commit, framed by Fieldnote 12's drawn lock board and its "same commit" join. Claim 2 is the take's local loop, recorded for this episode: `spi service pin partition --source`, `spi service list`, `spi test --source` on a clean tree (matched), the same run on a dirty tree (unmatched), and `spi service reset`. A strip across the top of claim 2 draws where each step lands: your checkout, the environment's registry, the image lock, the pod. Everything stays at level 200: real commands, their output, the usual path, and one failure, a pass that is labeled unmatched and so proves nothing about the commit the pod runs. The verdict script, `--report`, `--review` and the myth `passed-means-checked` are S302's; borrowing is the platform path's.

**Made from:** Fieldnote 12 v1, re-cut. Kept: claim 1's lock board, its pull pane and its "same commit" join, now fed from the new take. Gone: the standfirst, the verdict and report beat, the drawn pin board (replaced by footage), the traced example (it retold claim 1 hop for hop), the easy mistake, the season close, and the cold open: the episode opens on the prequel. Every line is a new take.

- Question: "How do I prove a change before a pull request?"
- Claim 1, "spi test runs the suite the deployed commit shipped": "When spi up resolves a service, the lock also records the acceptance image built from the same commit; once the environment is Deployable, spi test pulls that image by digest and runs its suite against your services, and the verdict names the image."

## Take notes

- **Footage:** the service path take day, part 2, recorded 24 October 2026 with spi 0.32.2 against a throwaway core environment (`take-day-service-path/footage`, the `s5-*` casts; `times.log` has the timings). Masked by the piece's `source/take/extract.py` into `source/take/lines.js`: the environment name and the cluster context as `<name>`, the environment's registry as `<registry>`, digests to eight hex characters, the suite container and the pod's hash suffix as `…`. The stack version the CLI prints after `<name>` (`0.32.1+448e0c65c7ee`) and the pin's timestamp column are cropped or left as printed; no date is narrated.
- **The deployed run** is the same commit and acceptance digest as Fieldnote 12's 0.32.0 take (`8aa26ca007ad`, `sha256:7fbfeeed…`); the new take replaces that footage so every pane in the episode is from one environment.
- **The pinned build** is the clean checkout of `8aa26ca007ad`, the deployed commit, not a code change: the episode says "a local build of your checkout's commit" over it and never claims the pin carried an edit. The edit is `README.md`, made after the pin, so the second `--source` run is unmatched.
- **Both `--source` runs passed** (11 tests, 1 skipped). The only difference is the provenance label, `matched` against `unmatched`; the narration never says the second run failed. It is the episode's one failure because its pass proves nothing about the commit the pod runs.
- **Claims checked** at osdu-spi-stack `origin/main` 44c44b8 (24 October): the paired acceptance digest in the lock and the pull by digest (`src/spi/testing.py`, `src/spi/images.py`); checkout mode's label, `matched` when the checkout's HEAD is the deployed commit and `git status --porcelain` is empty, `unmatched` otherwise (`checkout_label` in `src/spi/testing.py`; ADR-036; `docs/design/fork-deployment.md`); the deployed commit of a local pin is the pin's source commit (`deployed_fork_commit`); `spi service pin --source` runs Maven on the host, `az acr run` into the environment's registry under `local/<service>`, pins by digest, and waits until the service runs the build (`src/spi/build.py`, `service_pin` and `_await_local_rollout` in `src/spi/cli.py`, ADR-038); `spi service reset` restores the canonical image (`service_reset`).
- The Deployable gate is named, not taught (S201). `spi info`'s tag is S204's.
