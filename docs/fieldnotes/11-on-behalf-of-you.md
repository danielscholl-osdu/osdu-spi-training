# Fieldnote 11 · On behalf of you

**Status: storyboard, revised after the take.** Season 2, _Working the stack_. Cut from osdu-spi-stack ADR-044 (`docs/decisions/044-core-profile-dags.md`), `software/stacks/osdu/dags/README.md`, and PRs #397, #401 and #402, read at `main` 3d3106d (release 0.32.0), and checked live on the take day's 0.32.0 environment. The lesson is `core-dags` in the _Working the stack_ book; the cut-from record goes into `src/content/videos.js` like season 1's.

**Who is watching:** an engineer who has brought up a core environment (Fieldnote 02) and has added themselves with `spi users` (Fieldnote 09). **What they should believe after it:** the stack decides which DAG files Airflow runs and refuses anything it can't verify; a Deployable environment is not yet proof that ingestion works; and every task writes on behalf of the person who started the run, which `spi test dags --as me` proves end to end.

**Money shot:** the report page's record panel, the submitted record beside the stored one, `createUser` lit with the caller's id (masked to `<you>`), after the chain of three credentials has drawn.

**Form: mixed, result first.** The episode opens on the take's result, a record in Storage whose `createUser` is the person who ran the command, then peels back how it got there. Boards carry the mechanism (the catalog, the layers, the credential chain); footage carries the proof (the loader's log, the registration Job's log, `spi test dags --as me --report` and its page, and a two-record run where one record is skipped). It breaks season 1's formula on purpose: a cold open before the question, footage for four of the six beats, and a bed under the proof.

**Length:** about 4 minutes (estimate below).

## Angles considered

- **Stored as you** (chosen). Result first, mixed, about 4 minutes. Best for engineers who will run ingestion on their own environment.
- **Green, and nothing stored.** Mistake first, opening on a drawn green run with an empty Storage. Dropped: on 0.32.0 a run whose only record is skipped fails (osdu-airflow 0.30.9 fails the task rather than lose data silently), so the opening would show something the stack no longer does.
- **Follow one manifest.** One continuous drawing: a dot carries a one-record manifest from the CLI through Workflow, Airflow and the task into Storage, each hop adding its credential. Boards only, about 3 minutes. Kept as the drawing for claim 3.
- **Four DAGs, one hash.** The supply-chain story: catalog, pins, fail-closed pod start, why not git-sync. About 2.5 minutes, for operators who will edit the catalog. A candidate cut-down.

## Keep it true

- Two families, four DAG ids: `spi-smoke` (`Echo_DAG`, `airflow_monitoring`) and `core-ingestion` (`Osdu_ingest`, `manifest_ingestion`). Never say "the OSDU DAGs" or imply CSV, by-reference, WITSML, EDS, SEG-Y or DDMS DAGs load.
- Files arrive by a loader init container from a pinned commit and per-file SHA-256: not git-sync, not blob storage, not a custom image.
- Registration is a non-gating layer-6 Job. It registers system workflows and unpauses; it never deletes. Deployable does not imply registration or ingestion.
- Workflow injects the user id from the caller's token and refuses one in the body. Its Airflow login (`osdu-workflow`, role User) carries no OSDU authority.
- Only the scheduler runs as the OSDU workload identity, under LocalExecutor. Do not draw task pods.
- `on-behalf-of` is honored only when the OSDU identity is in `users.datalake.delegation` and the caller is in `users.datalake.impersonation`. Editor, admin and ops presets carry impersonation; viewer does not.
- On 0.32.0 (`osdu-airflow` 0.30.9), `Osdu_ingest` fails when Storage skips every record, or a whole manifest section, and finishes when it skips only some. Never say a run with nothing stored goes green. The two causes the preflight refuses are an invalid legal tag (via `legaltags:validate`) and an ACL whose domain differs from the caller's first group email. The check compares domains; it does not test membership.
- When Legal won't validate for the caller, that check is "not checked" and the smoke goes on. Don't say the check always validates the legal tag.
- No CI runs `spi test dags` against a live cluster. Never say "the pipeline proves ingestion".
- Times on screen are from the take, or labeled illustrative.
